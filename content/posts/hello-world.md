Welcome to my homelab and tech blog!

## What This Blog Is About

I'll be writing about:

- **Homelab experiments** — Synology NAS setup, networking, self-hosting services
- **DIY projects** — hardware builds, automation, home infrastructure
- **AI & Machine Learning** — experiments, tools, and notes from work
- **Software engineering** — things I learn and find worth sharing

## The Stack

This blog is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and hosted on my Synology NAS via Web Station.
Posts are written in Markdown, committed to GitHub, and deployed automatically via GitHub Actions.
