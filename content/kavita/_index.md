Kavita — the word means poem in both Marathi and Hindi, and carries within it the full weight of the lyric tradition: the close attention, the compressed feeling, the words that say more than they say.

This section reads Marathi and Hindi poems carefully — modern lyric verse from the great tradition of the twentieth century. Each poem is given in the original Devanagari, with Roman transliteration, word-by-word meanings, and an explanation of what the poet was doing with the language and why it stays with you.
